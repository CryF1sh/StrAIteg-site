FAQ

FAQ for manufacturers

Is it really free?

Yes. Equipment and vendor selection is free for manufacturers, with no charge of any kind. The service is paid for by vendors who work with requests in their account.

Do you sell equipment?

No. We don't sell equipment and don't compete with suppliers. Our job is to help you settle on the equipment and find who will supply it.

How do you find a vendor?

We match your task against vendor and integrator profiles: product range, references in similar projects and region. We suggest the ones that fit, and you choose who gets the spec.

Will vendors start calling me?

No. The selection comes to you. Only the suppliers you choose get your contact details.

What happens to my data?

Your task description is stored on servers in Russia and used only for your selection. We don't use your data for training and don't share it with third parties without your consent.

How accurate are the selection and the figures?

Every option comes with reasoning and a source link. Anything uncertain is marked "Needs expert review". Cost and payback are preliminary; the exact price comes in the vendor's quote based on your spec.

What if nothing fits?

We'll tell you so and explain why. If the task can be solved another way, for example with a different class of equipment, we'll suggest it.

What happens after I send a request?

Within one business day we message you in the channel you chose, clarify 2–3 details if needed and send the selection: a spec, 3 options and matching vendors.

Do I need to know anything about robots?

No. Describe the operation the way you'd explain it to a new employee: what you do, how much, what the constraints are. We'll work out the equipment parameters ourselves.
